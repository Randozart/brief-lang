## What Changed

- **Composite-node decomposition** (§11 of `docs/plans/2026-07-30-flat-node-decomposition.md`):
  - `analysis/match_normalize.rs` — statement-level `match` → `when` sequence (fallback = negation of all other arms, never `when true`)
  - `analysis/node_decompose.rs` — three-segment split at top-level `when` guards; static predicate classification (AlwaysTrue → inline, AlwaysFalse → drop, Runtime → two versions)
  - `counter.rs::emit_version_dag_main` — guard-absent loop + guard-present block, predicate evaluated at the split point (captures pre/post-increment naturally, no counter-name matching)
- **Minimal-state classification** (`analysis/loop_carried.rs`): loop-invariant fields hoisted, dead fields dropped, loop-carried fields get phis; hot loop has zero %State traffic.
- **Unconditional read-write conflict detection** (XOR rule) in `select_dispatch_mode` / `resolve_fusable_pairs`.
- **Faithful float printing**: `__print_float` `%g` → `%.9g` (round-trips float32).
- **Removed** the batch-loop heuristics (`loop_peeling.rs`, `BatchInfo`, `emit_countable_batched_main`).
